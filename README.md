### The problem I work on

A generative pipeline will produce a month of brand work in an afternoon, and most of it will be *nearly* right. A wordmark with the wrong bowl on the letter. A printed panel where the small type has been re-imagined into near-words. A statistic that is almost sourced. None of it looks wrong at a glance, and a glance is all anyone gives it.

Volume was never the hard part. Deciding what you are willing to put a client's name on is the hard part.

So my half of the studio is the layer that says no: the rules that make a failure mechanically catchable, and the systems that enforce them before a human ever sees the work.

### Systems

| | |
|---|---|

| **[figma-docgen](https://github.com/anishadogra/figma-docgen)** | Contracts, invoices and catalogs generated as native Figma documents. Half of it is a failure record: 500 lines of every mistake across four rebuilds and its specific fix. |
| **[brand-content-engine](https://github.com/anishadogra/brand-content-engine)** | Content production where research happens *before* copy. No line is written until a sourced research artefact exists, because a model with nothing to say will call anything seamless. |

### Rules

| | |
|---|---|
| **[brand-fidelity-rules](https://github.com/anishadogra/brand-fidelity-rules)** | Sixty-two rules that stop a generative step quietly replacing a brand's identity with something plausible and wrong. Every rule carries the incident that produced it. |
| **[decision-ledger](https://github.com/anishadogra/decision-ledger)** | A format for recording what a team rejected and why. The trick is that a rejection is written as a positive specification — kill the treatment, then lock the replacement in the next row. |

Also published, not pinned: [measurement-honesty](https://github.com/anishadogra/measurement-honesty), [plain-language-brief](https://github.com/anishadogra/plain-language-brief), [studio-portfolio-clearance](https://github.com/anishadogra/studio-portfolio-clearance).

### How I work

- **A rule without an incident is a preference.** Preferences are fine. They belong in a style guide, where disagreeing with one is cheap.
- **The reviewer cannot be the author.** Any check that can fix what it finds stops being a check.
- **A number carries its tag or it does not ship.** Confirmed, proxy, cannot-confirm, target. A proxy quietly promoted to a result is how an honest report becomes a dishonest one.
- **Publish the refutation.** The research repos name what was disproved, including where it contradicted the brief that paid for it.
